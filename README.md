# Showroom TANGO

**2-Player Top-Down Bullet-Hell | Unity 6 + FishNet | SRH Heidelberg | 2026**

Survival becomes a partner dance — coordination matters more than perfect execution. Two players navigate a bullet-hell arena where staying in sync is the only way to make it out.

## What I built

- Full 2-player networked game in Unity 6 using FishNet for multiplayer
- Top-down bullet-hell mechanics with cooperative survival loop
- Title sequence animation created in Adobe After Effects (80s arcade energy, neon typography, prismatic effects)

## Tech

- **Engine:** Unity 6
- **Networking:** FishNet
- **Animation:** Adobe After Effects (title sequence)
- **Solo project** — game design, implementation, motion design

## Context

Academic project at SRH Heidelberg, Game & Multimedia Design programme, 2026.
